Our Mission

To encourage meaningful learning, intellectual development and the application of knowledge through education in mathematics and engineering-related fields.

◉

Our Vision

To foster a learning environment that inspires curiosity, academic excellence and the development of individuals capable of contributing to society through knowledge and innovation.

OUR BACKGROUND

Our History

The Department of Mathematics & Engineering forms part of the academic community at Northern Caribbean University.

Through education and academic development, the department focuses on helping students strengthen their understanding of mathematical concepts and engineering principles.

The department continues to provide opportunities for learning, critical thinking and the development of skills that can be applied to real-world challenges.

Its academic environment encourages students to explore new ideas, develop their abilities and prepare for future opportunities.
