A LITTLE ABOUT US

Getting To Know Our Department

Welcome to the Department of Mathematics & Engineering at Northern Caribbean University. The fields of mathematics and engineering are closely connected to the way we understand and interact with the world around us.

Through the study of mathematical principles and engineering concepts, students are encouraged to think critically, approach challenges creatively and develop practical problem-solving skills.

Our aim is to support an environment where learning goes beyond the classroom and encourages students to explore new ideas and possibilities.

Our Mission & Visions

Our Mission
