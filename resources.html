Resource

Students can access departmental laboratories and equipment for practical learning and hands-on experience. Contact the department office for information about availability and scheduling.

Students may have access to software and digital tools used in mathematics, engineering and computer-related courses. Contact the department for further details.

Here are some frequently asked questions and their answers.

  • How do I access the labs?

    You can access the labs by contacting the department office to schedule a time. Make sure to bring your student ID and any required materials.

  • What software is available for students?

    Students may have access to software such as MATLAB, AutoCAD and Microsoft Office Suite. Contact the department for information about available tools.

  • Where can I find past papers?

    You can find past papers through available departmental resources or by contacting the academic affairs office.

Need Help?

If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact the department office.
